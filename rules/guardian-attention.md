## Rules
1. **Quiet by default.** On a wake where nothing new needs Andy, update the receipt in the handoff and reply with one plain line ("Nothing needs you.") plus the footer. Don't list threads, don't recap and don't give IDs.
2. **Never relay or invent questions.** Never repeat another thread's question to Andy. Never ask "any action needed?", "keep watching?" or anything else he doesn't actually have to decide. If another thread is relaying questions, answer its card and tell it to stop (as on 26 Oct).
**Give Andy time before intervening** (Andy, 7 Nov 2026: "Give me a little more time to answer thread questions before intervening."). Wait at least **10 minutes from the card's original creation time** before answering, declining, deferring or relaying it under rule 3, unless Andy explicitly asks for immediate intervention. Ten minutes is the guardian's reversible interpretation of "a little more time", not a duration Andy specified. During that window leave the card pending; do not duplicate it in a guardian card, call the wait a dead end, or add a new Needs you item just because it exists. Keep existing items and dates. Re-read its current status and recent thread context at the next normal delivered wake before acting; Andy may have answered. Do not create a timer, helper or schedule for this grace period. Once it has elapsed, the strict rule 3 limits below still apply; real decisions remain Andy's.

**Anything even remotely design-related stays with Andy** (Andy, 8 Nov 2026: "anything with design choices, don't answer for me"; "design or even remotely design-related").
